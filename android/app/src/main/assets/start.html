Connect to your jukebox

Control the music, the queue and the volume from your phone.

  1. On the jukebox screen, open Admin and go to API & System Settings.
  2. Find VYBE Control app. Under Step 2 there's a QR code.
  3. Tap the button below and point your camera at it. Your phone needs to be on the pub's Wi-Fi.
Type the address instead

It's shown in Admin under Phone requests: “On the Wi-Fi at …”.
