    Pick a Roblox theme

    Each theme sets a font, a cursor, a death sound and an animated avatar background. Try one in the preview. Nothing changes in Roblox until you press Apply.

    My Roblox themes

    Themes you save show up here. Change anything, then press Save to My themes.

    Font

    Changes the text in Roblox's menus, chat and player list, and in games that use Roblox's own fonts.

    Any .ttf or .otf font works. Roblox uses one font for every weight, so bold text looks a little lighter.

    Cursor

    Hover over the preview to try a cursor with your own mouse.

    Your own picture points…

    Roblox clicks wherever the middle of the cursor picture is. Gel shrinks your picture to fit and lines it up so it clicks where it points.

    Death sound

    What you hear when your character dies. Press play to listen.

    Roblox only plays Ogg Vorbis sounds (.ogg). Short ones work best.

    Avatar background

    An animated background behind your character on the Avatar page of the Roblox app. It moves with Roblox's own particle effects, so there are no scripts.

    If you wear a background you got from the Marketplace, Roblox may show that picture behind the animation instead of Gel's colours.

    This is new. If the Avatar page looks wrong, pick Roblox's own here and apply again, or press Back to normal Roblox.

    Setup

    Gel themes Roblox by swapping the font, cursor and sound files Roblox loads when it starts. It doesn't touch your account or the games you play.

      What Gel can change

      The font, the cursor and the death sound. Menu colours are built into Roblox itself, so those stay as they are.

      Setup

      Gel themes YouTube through a small browser extension that Gel makes for you. It only works on youtube.com and doesn't touch your Google account.
